# Human Help Routing

LifeMode Companion must never trap a person inside AI when they need a human.

## Product rule

AI helps the person reach truth, completion, and the right human. It does not replace the human.

## Human Help tab

The LifeMode panel should eventually include:

- Steps
- Companion
- Memory
- Human Help

The Human Help tab should ask:

> What kind of human help do you need right now?

Options:

- I may hurt myself or I feel unsafe
- I need benefits or VA claim help
- I need a VA social worker or case support
- I need PTSD, TBI, or mental health support
- I need housing, food, legal, or financial help
- I need a trusted person to understand this page
- I need help preparing for an appointment

## Crisis support

For Veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and loved ones:

- Dial 988 then Press 1
- Text 838255
- Chat online through Veterans Crisis Line

Source: https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/

For civilians and anyone in emotional distress in the United States:

- Call or text 988
- Chat through 988 Lifeline

Source: https://988lifeline.org/

## VA accredited representative / VSO support

LifeMode should help Veterans find a VA-accredited representative or VSO.

The app should clearly say:

- accredited VSO representative services for VA benefit claims are always free
- accredited attorneys or claims agents may charge fees
- LifeMode is not a lawyer, VSO, or accredited representative
- LifeMode can create a handoff to bring to a VSO

Source: https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/

## VA social worker support

LifeMode should route users toward VA social work when the issue is bigger than a form.

VA social workers can help Veterans and families with resource navigation, crisis intervention, advocacy, case management, benefit assistance, mental-health-related support, substance-use treatment support, and housing instability or homelessness support.

Source: https://www.socialwork.va.gov/

## Companion boundaries

LifeMode Companion must not claim to be:

- a doctor
- a therapist
- a lawyer
- a VSO
- a claims agent
- a social worker
- a crisis counselor

It can help with:

- plain-language explanations
- next steps
- checklists
- draft descriptions
- questions for human helpers
- handoff notes
- evidence organization
- resource routing

## Required crisis wording

Use calm, direct language:

> If you may hurt yourself or someone else, call emergency services now. Veterans and loved ones can dial 988 then Press 1 or text 838255 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line. Anyone in the U.S. can call or text 988 for crisis support.
