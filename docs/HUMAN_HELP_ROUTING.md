# Human Help Routing

LifeMode Companion must never trap a person inside AI when they need a human.

## Product rule

AI helps the person reach truth, completion, and the right human. It does not replace the human.

## Human Help tab

The LifeMode panel should eventually include:

- Steps
- Companion
- Memory
- Human Help

The Human Help tab should help route to:

- urgent support resources
- benefits or claim help
- social worker or case support
- PTSD, TBI, or mental health support
- housing, food, legal, or financial help
- trusted person handoff
- appointment preparation

## Resource links to verify

- Veterans Crisis Line: https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/
- 988 Lifeline: https://988lifeline.org/
- VA accredited representative search: https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/
- VA Social Work: https://www.socialwork.va.gov/

## Companion boundaries

LifeMode Companion must not claim to be:

- a doctor
- a therapist
- a lawyer
- a VSO
- a claims agent
- a social worker
- a crisis counselor

It can help with:

- plain-language explanations
- next steps
- checklists
- draft descriptions
- questions for human helpers
- handoff notes
- evidence organization
- resource routing

## Required design principle

Use calm, direct language and route to official human support whenever the need is beyond task support.
