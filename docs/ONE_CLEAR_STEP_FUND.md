# The One Clear Step Fund

## Purpose

The One Clear Step Fund exists to keep LifeMode Veteran free for Veterans while still funding the real cost of privacy, support, infrastructure, accessibility, AI assistance, and human-help routing.

## Donation promise

A donation is not for an app.

A donation is for the moment before someone gives up on the task.

A donation helps a Veteran reach the next page, the next document, the next appointment, the next VSO, the next social worker, the next trusted person, or the next safe step.

## What donations fund

Donations and sponsorships can fund:

- free LifeMode Veteran access
- LifeMode Companion credits for Veterans
- VA claim organization mode
- application check mode
- document checklist support
- memory support
- crisis and human-help routing
- privacy and security infrastructure
- accessibility testing
- outreach to Veteran organizations
- training material for partners
- StoryBridge and Witness Wall safety review

## Sponsor tiers draft

### One Clear Step Sponsor

Funds access for individual Veterans.

### Companion Sponsor

Funds advanced AI Companion support for Veterans.

### County Bridge Partner

Funds local workflows for county veteran offices or local Veteran-serving organizations.

### Clinic Bridge Partner

Funds workflows for clinics, social workers, care coordinators, and case-support teams.

### Employer Bridge Partner

Funds transition support for Veteran employees and military families.

### Foundation Bridge Partner

Funds large-scale Veteran access, research, safety review, and outreach.

## Transparency promise

LifeMode should publish plain-language impact reporting:

- number of Veterans sponsored
- workflows supported
- partner organizations onboarded
- basic usage outcomes without exposing private content
- stories published with permission
- safety improvements made

## What should never happen

Do not:

- sell user data
- exploit Veteran stories for fundraising
- lock crisis resources behind payment
- pressure vulnerable users to donate
- make false promises about outcomes
- hide the limits of AI
- publish private content without consent

## Mission sentence

> The Veteran gets the bridge. Sponsors fund the bridge.
