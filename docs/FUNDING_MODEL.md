# LifeMode Funding Model

LifeMode must be financially sustainable without betraying the mission.

## Revenue principle

The person in the most pain should not be the person we squeeze.

Veterans get LifeMode Veteran free.
Organizations, sponsors, donors, and civilian paid plans fund the work.

## Free forever layer

For Veterans, the following should stay free:

- core extension
- one clear next step
- checklist generation
- form rescue basics
- focus mode
- read aloud
- memory note
- copy handoff
- crisis and human-help routing
- trusted-person handoff
- VSO and VA social worker routing

## Sponsored Pro layer

Sponsors can fund advanced features for Veterans:

- LifeMode Companion AI
- VA Claim Companion
- application check
- description drafting
- evidence checklist
- appointment preparation
- deadline memory
- saved workflows
- document binder
- caregiver and VSO handoff

Sponsors may include:

- nonprofits
- county veteran offices
- VA-adjacent support organizations
- clinics
- employers
- universities
- foundations
- donors
- grant programs

## Organization layer

Organizations pay for better workflows and support:

- custom workflows
- partner landing pages
- admin install support
- staff training materials
- non-sensitive usage analytics
- accessibility reports
- workflow templates
- case-worker handoff tools

## Civilian paid layer

Civilian users may have a free basic version and paid Pro options:

- housing application support
- medical portal support
- job application support
- insurance and benefits forms
- school accommodation workflows
- elder care and caregiver workflows
- document binder
- appointment preparation

## Donation layer

Create a fund to sponsor access:

# The One Clear Step Fund

Purpose:

- keep LifeMode Veteran free
- fund Companion Pro for Veterans
- support outreach
- pay for security, privacy, and accessibility work
- support trusted partner onboarding

## What a donation buys

A donation is not only for software.

A donation buys time for someone who is running out of it.

It helps one person reach the next step before the page breaks them.
