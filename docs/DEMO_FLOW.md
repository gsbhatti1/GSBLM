# Sprint 4 Demo Flow

## Goal

Show LifeMode as a working Companion, not only an accessibility widget.

## 60-second demo story

LifeMode is for the moment when a page feels too heavy. It turns the current page into steps, memory, and human help so the person does not lose the thread.

## Demo script

1. Open `https://www.va.gov`.
2. Click the LifeMode icon once.
3. Say: "LifeMode opens directly. No launcher popup."
4. Point to the page type badge.
5. Say: "It detects this as a task portal."
6. Show the link badges on the page.
7. Click **Start first step**.
8. Click **Go** on a checklist item.
9. Say: "It does not just summarize. It points to where to act."
10. Open **Companion**.
11. Click **Explain this page**.
12. Click **Check what I am missing**.
13. Open **Memory**.
14. Type: "Call VSO tomorrow about claim status."
15. Save note.
16. Open **Human Help**.
17. Show 988, Veterans Crisis Line, VSO, VA Social Work, local ER, urgent care, 211, SAMHSA, and trusted person message.
18. Refresh page.
19. Show LifeMode reopens.
20. Close with X.
21. Refresh again.
22. Show LifeMode stays closed.

## Closing line

No one should be left alone with the page that breaks them.
