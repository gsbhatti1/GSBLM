# Human Help Resource Map

This file tracks official support resources that LifeMode may route users toward.

Always verify these links and phone/text details before publishing or shipping.

## Crisis support

### Veterans Crisis Line

Use for Veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, families, friends, and supporters who need immediate crisis support.

Current public contact details to verify:

- Dial 988 then Press 1
- Text 838255
- Chat online through Veterans Crisis Line

Source:

- https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/

### 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline

Use for civilian crisis support and anyone in emotional distress.

Current public contact details to verify:

- Call 988
- Text 988
- Chat through the official 988 Lifeline website

Source:

- https://988lifeline.org/

## Veteran claim and benefits help

### VA accredited representatives and VSOs

Use when a Veteran needs help filing a claim, appointing a representative, or requesting a decision review.

Important note:

- VA states accredited VSO representative services for VA benefit claims are always free.
- Accredited attorneys and claims agents may charge fees.

Source:

- https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/

## VA social work

Use when a Veteran may need help with access to care, resources, housing, benefits, caregiver support, counseling, or case management.

Source:

- https://www.socialwork.va.gov/

## Wounded Warrior Project

Use as a referral option for eligible post-9/11 wounded, ill, or injured veterans, service members, and their families.

Source:

- https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/
- https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/programs/warrior-care-network

## Product rule

LifeMode can suggest support resources, but it must not claim to be emergency care, licensed clinical care, legal representation, VA-accredited claims representation, or a replacement for a human helper.
