# StoryBridge / Witness Wall Design

## Purpose

StoryBridge brings real, sourced stories of service, transition, grief, paperwork, pain, survival, and help-seeking into LifeMode without exploiting trauma.

## Public section name

The Witness Wall

## Engine name

StoryBridge

## Rule

Every story must become a bridge back to help.

## Source policy

Use trusted public sources and link back to the original source. Do not copy full articles. Do not rewrite private trauma without consent. Do not sensationalize suicide or describe graphic methods.

## Story card

- title
- source
- original link
- public name if already public or permission granted
- branch / community when available
- themes
- system gap
- what helped
- LifeMode reflection
- one clear next step
- human help routes

## Themes

- transition
- PTSD
- TBI
- chronic pain
- VA claims
- homelessness
- caregiver burden
- survivor guilt
- grief
- moral injury
- Memorial Day
- family strain
- asking for help after being trained to stay tough

## Output format

1. What happened
2. What the system made hard
3. What this story teaches
4. One clear next step
5. Help now
