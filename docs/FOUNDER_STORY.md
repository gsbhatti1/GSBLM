# Founder Story

LifeMode began from a simple daily-life truth:

> I know what I need to do, but my brain cannot get me through the steps.

This is not a small problem. It shows up when a person is staring at a form, a benefits page, a housing application, a medical portal, a job application, a lease, or a long list of instructions.

They are not lazy. They are not broken. They are overloaded.

LifeMode exists for that moment.

## The human we serve

The first user may be a combat veteran, a person with ADHD, a person recovering from TBI, a person living with PTSD, a caregiver, a parent, a student, or someone carrying too much at once.

They may open a page and freeze.

LifeMode should quietly say:

> Let's take one step.

## The promise

LifeMode does not try to fix a person.

LifeMode helps the person finish the next task life is asking from them.

The product turns confusing pages into:

- plain language
- a short checklist
- one suggested next step
- read-aloud support
- focus mode
- a calmer page

## The first rescue

The first version does not need a giant platform.

It needs to help one real person tomorrow.

Success means someone who was stuck can say:

> I finished the step I could not start.

## Build with respect

LifeMode must protect the user.

No shame. No pressure. No confusing dashboard. No unnecessary account. No hidden data collection.

The product must earn trust by being useful, calm, and careful.

## North star

One human. One stuck moment. One clear next step.
