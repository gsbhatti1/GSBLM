# LifeMode Veteran Promise

This is a company law.

## The promise

**LifeMode Veteran is free for Veterans. 100%.**

Not a free trial.
Not a discount.
Not the weak version.
Not a paywall before help.

The Veteran is not the revenue target.
The Veteran is the reason.

## Why this exists

A Veteran should not have to enter a credit card before getting help with the page, form, portal, appointment, or claim process that is breaking them down.

LifeMode exists for the stuck moment:

> I know what I need to do, but my brain cannot get me through the steps.

The first mission is not scale.
The first mission is not valuation.
The first mission is not press.

The first mission is:

> Save one human.

That human may be someone's father, mother, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, son, daughter, spouse, caregiver, friend, or battle buddy.

## What stays free for Veterans

The Veteran version should include, at minimum:

- one clear next step
- page and form checklist
- focus mode
- read aloud
- memory note
- copy handoff
- basic missing-field check
- crisis and human-help routing
- VSO handoff support
- VA social worker routing
- trusted-person handoff

## What can be funded by sponsors

Advanced features can be sponsored rather than charged to Veterans:

- LifeMode Companion AI
- saved workflows
- document binder
- VA claim companion mode
- application review
- description drafting
- appointment preparation
- deadline memory
- trusted-helper sharing
- case-worker handoff

## Funding model

The Veteran gets the bridge.

The bridge is funded by:

- nonprofits
- county veteran offices
- clinics
- grants
- employers
- donors
- foundations
- organization plans
- workflow support contracts
- civilian paid Pro plans

## Core rule

If a decision makes LifeMode more profitable but makes a Veteran less protected, choose the Veteran.

## Line to keep forever

> No Veteran pays for the bridge back to help.
