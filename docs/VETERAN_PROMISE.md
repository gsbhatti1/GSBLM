# LifeMode Veteran Promise

## Company law

> LifeMode Veteran is free for Veterans. 100%.

Not a free trial.

Not a discount.

Not a limited version that blocks the real help.

Veterans are not the revenue target.

Veterans are the reason.

## Why this exists

LifeMode exists because many people know what they need to do, but the page, form, portal, appointment, or process is too heavy to move through alone.

For Veterans, this can show up in the hardest places:

- VA claims
- disability forms
- C&P exam preparation
- medical portals
- benefit letters
- housing applications
- job transition
- family support
- crisis routing
- social worker connection
- VSO handoff

A Veteran should not be asked for a credit card before getting help with the page that may decide care, benefits, housing, medication, or the next step back to support.

## Free for Veterans

The LifeMode Veteran layer should include free access to:

- one clear next step
- page and form checklist
- focus mode
- read aloud
- copy handoff
- memory note
- human help routing
- crisis resource routing
- VSO and accredited representative routing
- basic application check
- basic VA claim organization

## Sponsored Pro

Advanced features may cost money to operate, but the Veteran should not carry that burden when possible.

LifeMode Veteran Pro should be funded through:

- nonprofits
- county veteran offices
- clinics
- grants
- employers
- donors
- sponsors
- veteran-serving organizations
- organization plans
- workflow support contracts

## Civilian and organization revenue

The mission can be sustained by charging where it is appropriate:

- civilian Pro plans
- organization dashboards
- custom workflows
- partner landing pages
- training and onboarding
- accessibility reports
- workflow analytics without private content
- implementation support

## Hard boundaries

LifeMode must not:

- sell Veterans' sensitive personal data
- charge Veterans for crisis routing
- put crisis resources behind paywalls
- promise VA claim outcomes
- act like a lawyer, doctor, therapist, licensed social worker, or VA-accredited representative
- push users to exaggerate or hide facts
- replace human care when human care is needed

## Funding statement

The Veteran receives the bridge.

Sponsors, partners, organizations, donors, and civilian paid plans fund the bridge.

## Mission sentence

> No Veteran pays for the bridge back to help.
