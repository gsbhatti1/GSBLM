# StoryBridge and The Witness Wall

## Purpose

StoryBridge is the system that brings real, sourced Veteran stories into LifeMode with dignity and safety.

The public experience is called:

> The Witness Wall

Subtitle:

> Real stories of service, survival, grief, paperwork, transition, and the fight to stay alive.

## Core rule

> Every story must become a bridge back to help.

Stories are not content to exploit.

Stories are not trauma marketing.

Stories are not proof that pain is entertaining.

A story belongs in LifeMode only if it helps a reader feel less alone, understand the struggle, and find one next step toward human support.

## What StoryBridge searches for

StoryBridge should search trusted public sources for themes like:

- transition from service
- PTSD
- TBI
- chronic pain
- memory problems
- difficulty concentrating
- social isolation
- grief after losing a fellow service member
- VA claim confusion
- benefit paperwork
- C&P exam fear
- housing instability
- family strain
- caregiver burden
- military sexual trauma
- moral injury
- survivor guilt
- women Veterans
- rural Veterans
- post-9/11 Veterans
- Vietnam-era Veterans
- active duty and Guard/Reserve support

## Trusted source lanes

Use public, reputable sources first:

- Make the Connection
- Library of Congress Veterans History Project
- StoryCorps Military Voices Initiative
- Wounded Warrior Project stories
- The War Horse
- VA stories and official Veteran support pages
- nonprofit partner stories with permission
- direct submissions with explicit permission

## Source card format

Each story needs a source card:

```text
Title:
Public name:
Branch:
Era or conflict:
Source publisher:
Original link:
Published date:
Permission status:
Themes:
Support resources connected:
Summary written by LifeMode:
Safety review status:
```

## Permission status

Use one of these:

```text
PUBLIC_SOURCE_LINK_ONLY
PUBLIC_SOURCE_SUMMARY_ALLOWED
DIRECT_PERMISSION_GRANTED
FAMILY_PERMISSION_GRANTED
DO_NOT_REWRITE
DO_NOT_USE
```

Default to caution.

If permission is not clear, do not republish the story. Link to the original source and write only a short original reflection.

## LifeMode reflection format

Each story page should include:

```text
Why this story matters
What the hidden struggle is
What system gap appears
What helped or could help
What LifeMode would do in this situation
One clear next step
Human help options
Original source link
```

## Suicide and grief safety rules

Stories involving suicide loss must be handled carefully.

Do not:

- describe the method
- describe the exact location in a way that could be harmful
- include graphic details
- include suicide note contents
- sensationalize the death
- romanticize the death
- make suicide appear heroic, honorable, inevitable, or simple
- reduce the story to one cause
- write headlines that shock or exploit

Do:

- use careful language such as "died by suicide"
- keep personal details general unless permission is explicit
- emphasize that help, coping skills, treatment, and connection can work
- include crisis resources
- route the reader to human help
- show respect for family, friends, and unit members

## Required help block

Every Witness Wall story page must include a help block.

For Veteran crisis support:

```text
If you are a Veteran, service member, Guard/Reserve member, family member, friend, or supporter and need support now:
Call 988 then Press 1.
Text 838255.
Chat with Veterans Crisis Line.
You do not need to be enrolled in VA benefits or VA health care to connect.
```

For civilian crisis support:

```text
Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
```

Also include task-specific help when relevant:

- Find a VSO or accredited representative
- Contact a VA social worker
- Find a Vet Center
- Contact Wounded Warrior Project Resource Center
- Send a handoff to a trusted person

## Product connection

StoryBridge should connect directly into LifeMode Companion.

Example:

```text
You are on a VA disability claim page.
This can feel heavy. Other Veterans have struggled with this step too.
Read a story like yours.
Then come back to your next step.
```

The story should not distract the user from the task. It should help them feel less alone and return them to the task with more strength.

## Companion prompts from a story

At the end of a story, offer:

- Help me find my next step
- Help me talk to someone
- Help me prepare for a VA claim
- Help me write what I am feeling
- Help me make questions for my VSO
- Help me send this to a trusted person

## What not to build

Do not build:

- a social feed
- comments under trauma stories
- ratings or reactions on suicide-loss stories
- anonymous unmoderated submissions
- AI-generated fake Veteran stories
- story scraping without source links
- rewritten full articles without permission

## North star

> The story is not the destination. The story is the bridge.
