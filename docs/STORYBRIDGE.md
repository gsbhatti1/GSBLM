# StoryBridge and The Witness Wall

StoryBridge is the story system for LifeMode.

The public-facing section is called:

# The Witness Wall

Subtitle:

> Real stories of service, survival, grief, paperwork, transition, and the fight to stay alive.

## Purpose

The purpose is not content marketing.
The purpose is not trauma entertainment.
The purpose is not using someone's pain for clicks.

The purpose is:

> Show the reader they are not alone, then bridge them back to help.

## Core rule

Every story must become a bridge back to help.

## Source lanes

StoryBridge may collect story references from trusted public sources such as:

- VA Make the Connection: https://www.maketheconnection.net/stories-of-connection/
- Library of Congress Veterans History Project: https://www.loc.gov/programs/veterans-history-project/about-this-program/
- StoryCorps Military Voices: https://storycorps.org/discover/military-voices/
- Wounded Warrior Project stories: https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/mission/meet-a-warrior
- The War Horse: https://thewarhorse.org/

## Ethical rules

StoryBridge must not:

- steal private stories
- copy full articles
- rewrite someone's life as if it belongs to us
- use a dead person's story carelessly
- include graphic suicide details
- sensationalize trauma
- make suffering look romantic
- use stories without source links
- hide the original source

StoryBridge must:

- link to the original source
- clearly name the source
- use names only when they are already public or permission is given
- summarize carefully
- add LifeMode reflection, not replacement
- include help resources
- follow safe suicide reporting principles

Safe reporting reference: https://reportingonsuicide.org/recommendations/

## Story card format

Each story should become a source card:

```text

Name:
```

## LifeMode reflection format

The LifeMode version should be short and original:

```text
Why this story matters:
The hidden pain:
The system gap:
Where LifeMode could have helped:
One clear next step for the reader:
Where to get human help:
```

## User journey

A user reads a story and sees:

- I am not alone.
- This struggle is real.
- Someone else survived something like this.
- Here is the next step.
- Here is a human who can help.

## Companion integration

StoryBridge should connect with LifeMode Companion:

- Do you relate to this story?
- Help me find my next step.
- Help me talk to someone.
- Help me prepare my VA claim.
- Help me write what I am feeling.
- Help me send this to a trusted person.
