Appointment Preparation

Use this page to test Reading Mode, Companion questions, and handoff copying.

What to bring

Bring your ID, insurance card, medication list, recent records, and any notes about symptoms or concerns.

Questions to ask

Write down the main problem, when it started, what makes it worse, what helps, and what you want to understand before you leave.
